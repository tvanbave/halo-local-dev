# HALO Local contributor instructions

Use synthetic fixtures and the offline checks documented in README.md. Do not
connect to hardware, brokers or home services as part of development or CI.
Live testing requires the owner's explicit instruction and exact target scope.

Preserve upstream attribution. Never commit credentials, operational state,
private captures or home-specific deployment evidence. Do not automatically
repeat uncertain setters or discard journals/address reservations to bypass a
failed operation. Keep sequence allocation and exclusive writer checks intact.

Changes to code are not permission to deploy. Report exact validation performed
and remaining hardware limitations. Do not claim full ESP parity from offline tests.
